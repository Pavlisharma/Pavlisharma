## 🧠 Core Security Principles

- **Understand the root cause:** Identify why a vulnerability exists rather than relying solely on scanner output.
- **Validate before reporting:** Distinguish confirmed vulnerabilities from potential findings and false positives.
- **Assess real-world impact:** Evaluate confidentiality, integrity, availability, and relevant business consequences.
- **Recommend practical remediation:** Provide actionable fixes and verify them through retesting.
- **Test responsibly:** Perform security assessments only within authorized scopes and controlled environments.
